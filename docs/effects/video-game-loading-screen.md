# Video Game Loading Screen Effect

Source trend: Instagram Reel `DYQ2TyWAy22`, described in the research notes as a person or scene turning into a video-game menu/loading screen.

## Direction

- Treat the effect as a persistent layered composite, not a one-off transition.
- Start with normal moving footage and the intro caption.
- On the beat drop, switch to cinematic slow motion.
- Composite a fantasy main-menu header, tagline, logo, menu options, and animated selector over the footage.
- Keep source audio muted and use only the chosen background song.

## Visual Rules

- Use the uploaded menu reference as the taste target: gold serif title, centered `OF THE` line treatment, ornate divider, italic tagline, and small circular logo in the upper-right.
- Keep the overlay transparent over footage with soft edge darkening, grain, glow, and vignette.
- Keep the header compact and biased to the upper-left so the subject remains readable.
- Avoid heavy opaque panels, generic sci-fi HUDs, neon loading bars, or static title-card behavior.
- The selector should visit menu options in order and feel like it is clicking through a real game menu.

## QA

- Generate a contact sheet across the finished render.
- Inspect frames around the drop and each selector change.
- Check that footage keeps moving before the drop and becomes slow motion after it.
- Confirm output is vertical, source audio is absent, and the music fades out cleanly.
